Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about QuizMaster.

Account & Registration

Creating an account is free and easy! Click the "Register" button in the top navigation bar, fill in your name, email, username, and password, then click submit. You'll be logged in automatically and ready to start taking quizzes.

Currently, password reset can be handled by contacting our support team at support@quizmaster.com. We're working on adding a self-service password reset feature in an upcoming update.

At this time, username changes require assistance from our support team. You can update your profile information by contacting us directly. We're working on adding self-service profile editing in a future update.
Taking Quizzes

Each quiz has a set number of questions, a time limit, and a passing score. Browse departments, select a quiz, review its prerequisites, and click "Start Quiz". Answer all questions within the time limit and submit. You'll see your results immediately — including which answers were correct and which were wrong.

Scoring & Results

Each correct answer earns the number of marks defined for that quiz (shown on the quiz detail page). Your total score is the sum of marks for all correct answers. The pass/fail status is determined by comparing your score against the quiz's pass mark threshold.

Visit your Profile page (click your name in the navigation bar) to see all your past quiz attempts, scores, pass/fail status, and completion dates. You can also review individual quiz results with detailed answer breakdowns.

The leaderboard displays the top-performing quiz attempts across the platform. Rankings are based on scores, with ties broken by completion time. Only passed attempts appear on the leaderboard. Keep taking quizzes and improving your scores to climb the ranks!
Technical & Support

QuizMaster works best on modern browsers including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari. We recommend using the latest version of your browser for the best experience. JavaScript must be enabled for the quiz timer and interactive features to work properly.

Absolutely! QuizMaster is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. The interface automatically adapts to your screen size. However, for timed quizzes, we recommend a stable internet connection to ensure your quiz submission goes through before time expires.

If you experience a submission issue (e.g., page didn't load, network error), please contact our support team at support@quizmaster.com with your username and the quiz name. If the quiz allows retakes, you can try again. Our team can investigate and assist with any technical issues.
